registration form








A student registration form is used to collect important information from students who want to join a school, college, or course. It usually includes details such as the student’s name, email address, password, gender, and course selection. Registration forms help institutions keep records organized and make the admission process easier and faster. HTML can be used to create simple and interactive registration forms for websites.
